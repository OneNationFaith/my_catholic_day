# One Nation Faith Brand Bible

**Status:** Working brand standard
**Updated:** August 10, 2026

## Brand Name

**One Nation Faith**

Public-facing product name should use this wording consistently. The internal Flutter/repository lineage may still contain `my_catholic_day`, but that is not the consumer brand.

## Positioning

One Nation Faith is a practical Catholic companion.

It should feel:
- Catholic
- trustworthy
- reverent
- warm
- clear
- useful
- grounded
- modern without being trendy

It should not feel:
- gimmicky
- partisan
- sensational
- cluttered
- preachy for its own sake
- like a generic “inspirational” app with Catholic decoration added later

## Product Promise

**Do not merely tell Catholics something. Help them do it.**

The brand should support the product journey:

**Know → Understand → Live**

## Voice

Preferred voice:
- Plain English
- Warm but not saccharine
- Confident without pretending certainty where the Church permits options
- Respectful of sacred things
- Practical
- Concise when the user needs action
- More detailed when explanation matters

Avoid:
- unnecessary jargon
- scolding
- fake quotes
- invented Church rules
- presenting devotional preference as doctrine
- overclaiming authority

## Visual Foundation

Current app colors are canonical until intentionally revised:

- **Navy:** `#17324D`
- **Burgundy:** `#7A263A`
- **Gold:** `#C69A45`
- **Cream:** `#F7F2E8`

These values are currently defined in `lib/main.dart`.

### Intended visual character

- Navy: stability, seriousness, trust
- Burgundy: Catholic warmth and depth
- Gold: sacred accent and emphasis
- Cream: softer, more human background than stark white

Use gold as an accent rather than allowing it to dominate every screen.

## Logo

Current app asset:

`assets/images/onf_logo.png`

Rules:
- Keep the logo recognizable.
- Do not distort proportions.
- Do not casually replace it with temporary/generated marks in production.
- Maintain adequate surrounding space.
- Use versions that remain legible at app-icon and splash-screen sizes.

## Information Design

The interface should prioritize the user's next useful action.

Examples:
- Today's Readings should lead directly to the readings.
- Confession content should lead into preparation or a guided flow.
- Saint content should explain why the saint matters today.
- Liturgical labels should be accurate but not crowd out the human-readable explanation.

## Catholic Identity

Catholic identity should come primarily from truthful content, liturgical rhythm, sacramental life, Scripture, prayer, saints, and Church teaching—not from excessive religious ornament.

Sacred symbols should be used deliberately and respectfully.

## Accessibility and Simplicity

Design for ordinary users, including people who are not technically confident or theologically trained.

Prefer:
- clear hierarchy
- readable type
- obvious buttons
- comfortable spacing
- minimal unnecessary choices
- predictable navigation

## Family Character

The brand should remain appropriate for individuals, families, parish use, and people returning to the faith.

It should never rely on shock, fear, ridicule, or culture-war engagement to keep attention.

## Brand Decision Test

Before adding a visual or copy element, ask:

- Is it recognizably One Nation Faith?
- Is it reverent?
- Is it clear?
- Is it useful?
- Does it make the Catholic experience easier to understand or live?
